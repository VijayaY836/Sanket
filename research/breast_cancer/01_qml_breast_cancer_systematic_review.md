# Investigating the Application of Quantum Machine Learning in Breast Cancer: A Systematic Review

**Authors:** Shahrzad Kaveh, Elahe Arezi, Zahra Khedri, Solmaz Sohrabei  
**Published:** 2025, *Archives of Breast Cancer*  
**DOI:** 10.32768/abc.2025122130-142  
**Article:** https://www.archbreastcancer.com/index.php/abc/article/view/1060

## Why this paper matters
This is the best starting paper for a team entering the topic because it reviews the existing breast-cancer/QML literature rather than presenting only one algorithm. It discusses QSVM, QCNN and QNN approaches and connects QML with screening, diagnosis, prognosis, personalized management and possible drug-discovery applications.

## What it studied
The authors searched PubMed, Embase, Scopus and Web of Science and reviewed work involving AI/QML for breast-cancer management. The paper reports QSVM, QCNN and QNN among the commonly used quantum approaches.

## Main takeaway
The review is optimistic about QML, but its reported aggregate performance should not be interpreted as proof that quantum models outperform modern classical models in real clinics. The underlying literature is heterogeneous and much of it consists of small datasets, simulations and proof-of-concept experiments.

## Relevance to our project
Use this paper to build the **literature-review/background section** and to identify algorithm families worth reproducing:
- Quantum Support Vector Machine (QSVM)
- Variational/Quantum Neural Networks
- Quantum/quanvolutional CNNs
- Hybrid quantum-classical learning

## Questions to discuss as a team
1. Do we want to work on diagnosis, subtype prediction, prognosis, treatment response, or drug discovery?
2. Can we reproduce a published QML result and compare it fairly with strong classical baselines?
3. Are we solving a problem where quantum encoding could plausibly add value, rather than adding quantum computing only for novelty?

## Evidence level / caution
**Review / early-stage research. Not evidence of a quantum cure for breast cancer.**
