# The Diagnostic Value of Artificial Intelligence in Oral Squamous Cell Carcinoma: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis

**Topic:** AI/ML/DL diagnostic accuracy for OSCC  
**Source:** ScienceDirect  
**Article:** https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2468785525002150

## Why it matters to a quantum project

Before claiming that QML improves oral-cancer detection, your team needs to understand the performance and limitations of the broader AI literature.

This systematic review/meta-analysis examines diagnostic AI applied to OSCC.

## Clinical context

OSCC constitutes the dominant histological type among oral-cavity malignancies. Conventional assessment involves clinical examination and histopathological biopsy, while AI research has explored automated image interpretation.

## How to use this paper

Use it to answer:

- What kinds of oral-cancer datasets are commonly used?
- Which performance metrics matter clinically?
- How well do existing ML/DL systems perform?
- Where are external validation and generalization weak?
- What would QML actually need to improve?

## Project lesson

If a classical deep-learning model already achieves excellent discrimination, obtaining a similar accuracy with a quantum simulator is not automatically meaningful.

Your project should test a specific hypothesis such as parameter efficiency, small-sample behavior, robustness, multimodal fusion, or representation learning.
