## Why this belongs in the pack

A quantum-computing project should begin with the **clinical problem**, not with the algorithm.

This scoping review examines emerging methods for early detection of oral cancer and oral potentially malignant disorders (OPMDs).

## Project relevance

Use the review to identify where computational tools could realistically fit:

`screening`
→ `suspicious lesion`
→ `diagnostic assessment`
→ `biopsy/histopathology`
→ `clinical decision`

A smartphone QML system, for example, would most realistically be investigated as a **screening/decision-support tool**, not as a replacement for biopsy based only on an experimental classifier.

## Questions for your proposal

- Is your system screening or diagnosis?
- What is the consequence of a false negative?
- Should sensitivity be prioritized?
- How will uncertainty be communicated?
- Is the dataset representative of different devices/populations?
- Is patient-level separation used?
