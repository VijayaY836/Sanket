## Important: this is NOT quantum machine learning

This paper concerns **quantum dots (QDs)**.

Quantum dots are nanoscale semiconductor materials with quantum-mechanical optical properties. They are a completely different research area from quantum computers and quantum machine learning.

## Why include it?

Because searches for “oral cancer quantum” frequently return quantum-dot literature.

Your team needs to know the distinction so that these papers are not accidentally cited as evidence for QML.

## Oral-cancer relevance

Quantum dots have been investigated for uses including:
- fluorescent imaging
- biomarkers/probes
- cancer-cell visualization
- diagnostic sensing
- targeted therapeutic research

## Relationship to your QML project

Potentially, quantum-dot imaging could generate data that an AI system analyzes, but:

**quantum-dot imaging ≠ quantum machine learning.**

They could theoretically coexist in one research pipeline, but they are separate technologies.
