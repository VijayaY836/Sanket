# Quantum-Inspired Feature Optimization and Hybrid Quantum Vision Transformer for Automated Oral Cancer Diagnosis

**Authors:** Patibandla Sandhya Krishna, Subba Rao Peram  
**Published:** 27 August 2026  
**Journal:** Journal of Intelligent Decision Making and Information Science  
**Article:** https://www.jidmis.org/index.php/jidmis/article/view/2405

## Proposed system

The paper presents **QFO-HQViT**, combining:

- image preprocessing
- Quantum-Inspired Feature Optimization (QIFO)
- Hybrid Quantum Vision Transformer (HQViT)
- uncertainty classification
- clinical decision-support components

## Dataset information reported

The article reports patient-level analysis involving:
- **5,486 images**
- **794 patients**
- 2,677 malignant images
- 2,809 benign images

It describes independent validation/testing and external datasets.

## Feature reduction

The QIFO component reportedly reduces:
- 2,048 initial features
to
- 384 features

The authors report removal of approximately 53.1% redundant features.

## Reported metrics

The paper reports approximately:
- Accuracy: 95.6%
- Sensitivity: 96.4%
- Specificity: 94.8%
- F1: 95.7%
- Precision: 95.3%
- PR-AUC: 96.8%
- Cross-device-shift accuracy: 91.5%

## Why this is interesting

It moves beyond a bare accuracy score by considering:
- calibration
- uncertainty
- device shift
- clinical interpretation
- ablation analysis

Those are valuable ideas for your own project even if you choose a different model.

## Caution

Carefully inspect what components are genuinely quantum, what components are **quantum-inspired**, and whether experiments use actual quantum hardware or classical simulation.

## Evidence classification

**Direct oral-cancer hybrid/quantum-inspired imaging research.**
